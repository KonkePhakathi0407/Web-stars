What to Expect
100% Confidential · Safe Space

First time booking? Here's how your session will go — simple, supportive, and completely private.

1
Confirmation
You'll receive an email with your appointment details and a reminder 24 hours before.
2
Arrive & Check In
Arrive 5–10 minutes early at the wellness centre reception. Just bring your student card — no paperwork needed.
3
Your Session
Sessions are 50 minutes in a private room. You set the pace — there's no pressure to share anything you're not ready for.
4
Confidentiality
Everything discussed stays between you and your counsellor, in line with UJ's privacy policy.
5
Follow-Up
Your counsellor may suggest follow-up sessions or resources. You're always free to book again at your own pace.
