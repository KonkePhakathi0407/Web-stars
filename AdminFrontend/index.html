How It Works

Four simple steps to manage student wellness across your institution.

01

Secure Sign Up

Verify that you are a MindCare administrator or professional. Your credentials are reviewed to ensure student data remains protected at all times.

02

Wellness Check-ins

Complete daily check-ins to monitor student wellbeing and emotional state. Track mood trends and flag students who may need immediate support.

03

Smart Support

Book appointments, respond to student needs, and guide them to helpful resources. Coordinate with counselors across all campus locations.

04

Thrive Always

Provide continuous care and support throughout the student journey — from first year anxiety to graduation day confidence.
